How It Works

Four steps from skin file to finished art.

Step 1

Drop Skin and Pick Poses

Drop your skin PNG. BrandCraft maps it to the 3D rig and renders 8 portrait angles at once.

Step 2

Reroll Lighting and Colors

Hit reroll. Each click seeds new light angles and backdrop colors. Stop when you like what you see.

Step 3

Fine-Tune in the Editor

Adjust bone rotations and camera framing directly. The preview updates live at full 2048px resolution.

Step 4

Export High-Res PNGs

Export square profile cards with contact shadows, or pull transparent alpha cutouts for your video thumbnails.

What You Get

Profile avatars and YouTube channel art calibrated to exact platform specs.

Square Profile Avatars

Export at 1024, 2048, or 4096 pixels square. Includes custom directional lighting and optional risograph halftone grain.

YouTube Banner Suite

Full 2560×1440 channel art with dual character busts fitted directly inside YouTube's 1546×423 desktop and mobile safe strip.

Portable Share Codes

Every pose angle and lighting coordinate packs into one text string. Paste it into BrandCraft to duplicate the setup on any skin.
