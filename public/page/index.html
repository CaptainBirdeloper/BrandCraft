Studio Walkthrough

Four precision stages from raw skin texture to print-grade masterpiece.

Drop Skin & Pick Poses

Drop your skin PNG. BrandCraft maps it to the 3D rig and renders 8 portrait angles simultaneously with full 3-point illumination.

Reroll Lighting & Colors

Hit reroll. Each click seeds randomized key light angles, fill ratios, and backdrop duotones. Lock your favorite with one tap.

Fine-Tune in Editor

Directly manipulate bone rotations, camera focal depth, and risograph grain density. Every slider responds in realtime at full 2048px resolution.
